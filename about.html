Rooted in love

Poet, intuitive soul guide, creative facilitator & speaker, exploring the places where expression, intuition, connection & self-discovery come together.

The root

This isn’t about becoming someone else. It is about remembering more of who you already are.

Everything I create grows from one place: love is the root of our existence. Poems, gatherings, conversations, a table of paint in the open air: these are the branches.

I don’t hand people answers. I hold the space where their own can surface.
