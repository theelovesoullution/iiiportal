I am Cyndiii Deusa.

Soy Cyndiii Deusa.

A poet, intuitive soul guide, creative facilitator & speaker, exploring the places where expression, intuition, connection & self-discovery come together.

Through words, creative experiences, intimate gatherings & conversation, I hold spaces for people to listen inward, express what lives within them & reconnect with the parts of themselves asking to be seen.

Poeta, guía intuitiva del alma, facilitadora creativa y oradora, explorando los lugares donde la expresión, la intuición, la conexión y el autodescubrimiento se encuentran.

A través de las palabras, experiencias creativas, encuentros íntimos y conversación, sostengo espacios para que las personas escuchen hacia adentro, expresen lo que vive en ellas y reconecten con las partes de sí mismas que piden ser vistas.

This isn’t about becoming someone else.

Poesía Intuitiva

I Flow

“I flow through life & let the wind take me. I don’t try to push back or organize it to fit a specific timeline. I release it, trusting that it is going to align for the greatest good of mine. & allow it to connect me with everything I am meant to on my path, Protecting me from all that I am not meant to pass. Like some of those slithering snakes in the grass. I flow through life & I let the water take me with its current, & soak in every present moment, Flowing down the stream, Seeing the reflection of what having true peace & balance, with meaning in life, truly means. I flow.”

With Soul & Spirit,
